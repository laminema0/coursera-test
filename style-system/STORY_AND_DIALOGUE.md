# STORY_AND_DIALOGUE.md — Tone, Writing & Structure

## 1. Comedy formula

**Mundane domestic problem + absurd behaviour + two flatmates who are too
tired to be properly angry.**

- Stakes are tiny (dinner, curtains, a chore, an errand). Treat them as a
  big deal in a flat, weary voice.
- One character does something weird/irresponsible with total conviction;
  the other is exasperated but ends up going along with it.
- Visual joke in the background (smoke, mess, a disaster) that nobody
  over-reacts to.
- Payoff = a flat reversal ("It's delicious, man." / "Alright man.
  Whatever.") then cut to the next location.

## 2. Voice rules

- Short lines. 3–10 words. Questions answered with questions.
- Casual UK/Aussie-flavoured slang ("bloody", "wet wipe", "a curry at…").
- Swearing is rhythm, not aggression — used as filler/emphasis.
- **Verbal tic**: lines end with a signature address word ("man").
  Pick our own tic per show/character if we want to differentiate
  (e.g. "mate", "bro", "pal") — decide in the show bible.
- Trailing sentences ("why are you cooking a bloody curry at…") cut off
  by the other character.
- Long silences are funny — script them as `(beat)`.

## 3. Episode structure (short, 1:30–3:00)

| Act | Length | Content | Typical shots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0–20 s | Odd situation already in progress | Over-shoulder wide of the scene |
| Argument | 20–60 s | Shot/reverse-shot bickering | Close-ups, reactions |
| Turn | 10–20 s | The weird thing turns out fine/good | Wide two-shot |
| Callback / new problem | 30–60 s | A forgotten promise resurfaces | Close-ups, insert shot |
| Resolution | 10–20 s | Flat surrender ("Alright. Whatever.") | Close-up → reaction |
| Out | 8–15 s | Exterior establishing + title card | Exterior wide, title |

## 4. Script format

```
SHOT 07 — CU KYLE-TYPE (cone) — kitchen BG, smoke gray
  A:  Davey, what are you doing, man?        [M-flap, gesture: rub head]
  (beat — B blinks)
SHOT 08 — OTS WIDE — B on chair at stove, A head FG left
  B:  What does it look like, man!?          [arms lift pan]
```

Each line → one subtitle card (≤ 2 lines, ≤ 32 chars/line).
