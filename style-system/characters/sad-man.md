## Concept
A tired man in a long coat who has had enough. Only ever seen from behind.

## Silhouette
- Base shape: tall rounded **bean/thumb**, top leaning slightly left (slumped)
- Height: 1.94 widths body + legs to 2.32
- Signature odd feature: the **long coat is the body** — collar, belt and back vent drawn on the one shape
