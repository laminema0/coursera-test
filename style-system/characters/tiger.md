## Concept
A thin, sad tiger in human form, in a long coat and a hat. Silent. Only
ever seen from behind. **Keep him simple** — few lines, no extra detail.

## Construction (W = scale unit, origin top centre, total height 2.32W)
| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Build | thin human body — coat ~0.7W wide, narrow sloped shoulders |
| Head | round white head hanging low; droops 0.025W on each out-breath |
| Tiger marks | **3 short stripes** on the back of the head, **striped tail** (3 stripes) hanging out under the coat — that's all |
| Ears | small rounded ears poking out under the hat brim |
| Hat | brimmed hat, brim droops at the sides, plain crown |
| Coat | long, one collar line; nothing else |
| Arms | thin outlined sleeves, small round hands |
| Legs | single thin lines, small black shoes |

## Acting notes
- Coat hem flicks out on twos when a train passes
- Never turns around; we never see his face
